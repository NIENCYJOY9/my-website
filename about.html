Why Masbate?

Masbate is made of 3 big islands: Masbate, Ticao, and Burias. It is in the Bicol Region, but it feels like a different world. You can swim with manta rays in the morning, relax on a sandbar in the afternoon, and eat fresh seafood at night.

We started Tara, Masbate! because we want more Filipinos to discover our home province - not just Boracay or Palawan.

Travel Tips for Masbate

Best Time:
Dec - May, dry season, best for manta rays and island hopping

What to Bring:
Sunblock, rash guard, extra clothes, powerbank, cash - few ATMs on islands

How to Go:
Bus from Manila to Pilar, Sorsogon (10 hrs) + Ferry to Masbate City (2 hrs) OR Flight Manila to Masbate City (1 hr)

Must Try Food:
Fresh seafood, Laing, Bicol Express, Suman, and Ticao's dried fish
